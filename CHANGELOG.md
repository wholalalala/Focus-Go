# Changelog

## Short protocol update — 2026-09-30

New v2 assessment/training blocks contain 8–12 trials; assessments including practice and one retry never exceed 20 per task. GNG uses traffic-light SVG cues; SA uses a distinct star-observation SVG scene. Updated bilingual instructions, demonstrations, audio and version-bound AI content. Saved v1 sessions and reports remain readable/resumable; personal baselines are compared only within their protocol version. Export includes all version-specific baselines.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-29

Initial local-first demo: six versioned playable probes, practice gating, deterministic metrics, evidence views, bilingual parent/child UI, local repositories/export, bounded training, optional OpenAI-compatible content generation with semantic review, constrained reports and fallback. Experimental protocol: no norms, clinical validation or established training efficacy.

# Personal Baseline

The first completed ASSESSMENT for each assessment ID/version is stored as PERSONAL_BASELINE. Later assessments of that version are FOLLOW_UP and compared only to that child's first completed assessment of the same version. Starting v2 after v1 establishes a separate baseline; the first assessment never compares itself with a later one. Six domain metrics are presented independently. A skipped task remains explicitly insufficient; its missing metrics are not replaced by zeros or invented values.

The baseline identifies starting performance under the recorded version, device and circumstances. It is not a population percentile, clinical cutoff, diagnosis or prognosis. Changes can reflect familiarization, motor practice, circumstances and measurement noise. A direction of change is not evidence of treatment effect.

Only completed assessment sessions enter comparison. TRAINING results are stored separately in history/export and never establish or replace a personal baseline.

Completed assessments also persist an `AbilitySnapshot` with per-domain metrics and finding IDs. Recommendations select only domains with recorded descriptive patterns; absent a pattern, parents choose manually.

# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, constructive and welcoming. Protect children and private data. Harassment, discrimination, sexualized content, threats and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the repository maintainer using the private contact channel shown on their GitHub profile; do not publish personal information in an issue. If no private channel exists yet, do not post sensitive reports publicly.
